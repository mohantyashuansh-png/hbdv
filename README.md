# 🎂 Vaishu's Birthday Surprise — Setup Guide

> Built with love for the most special birthday ever 💖

---

## 📁 Folder Structure

```
New folder/
├── index.html       ← Main birthday app (open this in browser!)
├── style.css        ← All the styling
├── app.js           ← Game logic & page transitions
├── photos/          ← ADD HER PHOTOS HERE
│   ├── photo1.jpg
│   ├── photo2.jpg
│   ├── photo3.jpg
│   ├── photo4.jpg
│   ├── photo5.jpg
│   ├── photo6.jpg
│   ├── bonus1.jpg   ← 3 bonus photos in Game Prize gift
│   ├── bonus2.jpg
│   └── bonus3.jpg
└── videos/
    └── video1.mp4   ← ADD THE VIDEO HERE
```

---

## 🖼️ How To Add Photos

1. Open the `photos/` folder
2. Add your photos and **rename them exactly** to: `photo1.jpg`, `photo2.jpg` ... `photo6.jpg`
3. For bonus strip photos in the Game Prize: `bonus1.jpg`, `bonus2.jpg`, `bonus3.jpg`

> ✅ Supported formats: `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.png`, `.webp`

---

## 🎥 How To Add Video

1. Open the `videos/` folder
2. Add your video and rename it to `video1.mp4`

> ✅ Supported formats: `.mp4` (recommended)

---

## 💌 How To Customize Messages

Open `index.html` and find these sections to edit the love messages:

- **Birthday Gift message** → Search for `"Happy Birthday, Vaishu. 🎂"`
- **Game Prize message**    → Search for `"You played the game — and won it flawlessly."`
- **Photo captions**        → Search for `"Memory 1 💕"` etc.

---

## 🌐 How To Open / Share

**Option 1 — Local:**
Just double-click `index.html` to open in browser!

**Option 2 — Share online (free, easy):**
1. Go to [netlify.com/drop](https://app.netlify.com/drop)
2. Drag and drop the **entire "New folder"** onto the page
3. It gives you a link — send it to her!
